Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, erases your progress, so keep the app installed when you update your phone.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Progress is stored on each device, so it doesn't move automatically yet.

An ad didn't give me my reward.

Rewards are only given when the ad plays to the end. If no ad loads, try again later or check your internet connection. If you watched the whole ad and got nothing, email us with the time it happened.

Can I turn off the blood?

Yes. Open Settings in the game and turn Blood off.

Can I use a game controller?

Yes. Pair a Bluetooth controller with your device and press any button in the game.

How do I remove my name from the leaderboard?

Email us your leaderboard name and we'll remove it.

How do I reset my game?

Open Settings and tap Reset Progress. This can't be undone.
